ABOUT

My Story

My journey with beauty began long before Oshin Beauty Salon & Academy was created. What started as a simple love for makeup soon became a passion for helping people feel truly confident in their own skin. I still remember the first time I did a bridal look — seeing the joy and emotion in her eyes made me realize that makeup is so much more than colors and brushes. It is about trust, comfort, and creating a moment a person will never forget. With years of training and experience, I knew I wanted to build something more than just a salon. Oshin became a place where artistry meets elegance, but also where clients feel cared for like family. Every bride, every client, every student who walks through our doors carries a story — and my job is to bring that story to life with a touch of timeless beauty. This is not just my career, it is my calling. And with Oshin, I’m proud to share not just makeup, but an experience — luxurious, personal, and unforgettable.
